Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-02. The facility measures the economy, not you. This page states exactly what data exists, where it lives, and what would have to change before that changes.

What we collect

Nothing, server-side. OOZEMeter is a static site served by GitHub Pages. We operate no servers, no databases, and no accounts. We cannot see who you are, and we have built nothing that tries.

What stays on your device

The site uses your browser's local storage for three small conveniences, none of which leave your device or are visible to us:

  • Streak counter — the count of consecutive days you've checked the jar.
  • Boot ceremony flag — remembers you've seen the intro so it never replays.
  • Audio preference — whether you turned the laboratory audio on.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all three. No cookies are set by us.

Third parties we load

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and, like any web host, receives standard server logs (IP address, user agent) governed by GitHub's privacy statement. We have no access to these logs.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's two typefaces; your browser requests them from Google's servers under Google's privacy policy.

Links to data sources (FRED, EIA, the Federal Reserve, and others) and the "Post to X" button take you to those services under their own policies. Nothing is sent to them on your behalf until you choose to go.

What would change, and how you'll know

Two additions are planned and not yet active. Consistent with the facility's house rules, each will be disclosed on this page before it ships:

  • Analytics — when visitor measurement (Google Analytics 4) activates, this page will say so, name what it collects, and link an opt-out.
  • Email — if you subscribe to reports (double opt-in, once the mail line opens), your address will live with the email service provider named here at that time, used only to send the reports, with one-click unsubscribe.

Advertising

No advertising, ad networks, or trackers run on this site today. House rule, already public: if ads ever run, they appear on article pages only — never on the Jar — and this policy gains a consent section first.

Children

The site is general-audience economics education and collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the public repository, where the site's entire code and data pipeline are auditable. A direct contact address will be published on the About page when the facility's mailbox opens.

Changes

Changes to this policy are made by commit to the public repository — the full edit history is permanently inspectable. The effective date above moves when the policy does.
