Ward M data loading

This gauge does not affect the household Ooze Score. It contributes one-sixth of the experimental Ward M raw composite before Ward M calibration.

Why this gauge matters

Oil is embedded in transport, freight, petrochemicals, and production. Large sustained moves can spread through the economy before all household prices adjust, making it useful as an upstream market gauge.

What it measures

Daily Cushing, Oklahoma West Texas Intermediate spot price in dollars per barrel, aggregated to a monthly mean.

Published by the U.S. Energy Information Administration; FRED transports the daily series.

Interpret it without overclaiming

Read the level together with duration, product markets, refinery conditions, taxes, distribution costs, and the gasoline line in the household jar. Pass-through is incomplete and occurs with lags.

How it behaved in 2008

Historical basis: Historical incident files use the latest data available at retrieval: a current-vintage reconstruction, not the release-time view available in 2008. Revised series can change, and a terminal calendar month may be partial.

Incident File — 2008

WTI surged to record nominal levels before collapsing during the financial crisis. The same year demonstrated both inflationary pressure and demand destruction, so price direction requires context.

Reproduce the data path

  1. Retrieve daily DCOILWTICO observations from EIA or FRED transport.
  2. Discard missing days and calculate the monthly arithmetic mean.
  3. Apply the published nominal-dollar anchors and label that basis explicitly.
  4. For anchor research, compare nominal and CPI-adjusted histories without silently replacing the released method.

Limits

The current anchors are nominal dollars. Inflation changes what a fixed dollar price means over decades, and WTI can diverge from other crude benchmarks or retail fuels. Falling WTI can accompany collapsing demand during a recession, so a one-direction high-price scale can miss severe demand stress. The anchor-validation report therefore treats energy anchors as provisional evidence, not settled truth.

This gauge's provisional anchors are percentile-checked against its full history in the anchor-validation report.

Frequently asked questions

Why not use gasoline here?

Gasoline is already a direct household intake line. Ward M uses WTI as the upstream market signal and keeps the two roles distinct.

Are the anchors inflation-adjusted?

No. The released Ward M anchors are nominal and explicitly provisional. A real-price alternative requires versioned comparison and public rationale.

Does expensive oil always mean recession?

No. Prices can rise with strong demand or supply disruption; the gauge measures pressure, not cause or outcome.
