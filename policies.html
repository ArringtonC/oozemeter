Policies

The boring pages, published proudly. The Area 51 framing is fiction; everything on this page is not.

Editorial policy

OOZEMeter publishes two kinds of writing. Facility-drafted pieces (Specimen Reports, data summaries) are generated by OOZEBOT, the facility's internal editorial engine — deterministic templates over the collected data — every clause is driven by a number you can check in data/latest.json, and nothing is generated that the data doesn't support. Operator dispatches are written by a human and labeled as such. No piece of either kind may state a figure that doesn't trace to a cited public source.

Correction policy

When we get something wrong, the fix is visible, not silent. Corrections are noted on the affected page with what changed and when. Because the entire site lives in a public repository, every change to every page and every number is permanently inspectable in the commit history — our audit log is not a promise, it's a link.

Data policy

All inputs are public data from government and institutional sources (Federal Reserve, BLS via FRED, EIA, NY Fed), fetched by an automated collector that runs daily. The monthly headline score seals once per month when that month's jobs report and CPI land. Individual lines refresh on their own release schedules and carry their own as-of dates; a feed that goes quiet past its normal cadence is flagged STALE rather than silently reused. Historical scores are an ex-post reconstruction using the latest revised observations — when a source revision moves any published historical value by a point or more, the collector detects it and logs it publicly to data/revisions.json.

Before anything publishes, an integrity gate checks every value against physical plausibility ranges and verifies the calibration anchors still hold. If any check fails, the site keeps the previous good reading and the pipeline stops until a human investigates.

Methodology version history

The recipe is public, and so are its changes. If we change how the score is computed, it's recorded here — never silently.
