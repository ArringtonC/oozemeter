# Usability Review — 2026-08-27

A new-user audit of the deployed site, plus the implementation it produced.
Scope: first-visit experience, daily-check habit, historical/archive value.
Method: rendered the current build headlessly (desktop 1440px + mobile 390px),
read the front page, archive, market, explainer, indicator and article pages,
and audited the interaction code (`lab.js`, `archive.html`, `lab.css`).

## The three questions, answered honestly

1. **Would anyone come here?** For the daily check — yes; the hero answers
   "is the economy stressed, how much, and what changed?" in one glance
   (`25/100 · STICKY · −1 vs June · Calmer than 7 of every 10 months since
   2003`). For depth — it was the weak half: no search, line histories
   unpublished, and the past was one line on a chart.
2. **Would the past history help?** Half. The 23-year chart with recession
   shading, gap disclosure and no interpolation reads beautifully, but you
   could not drill into a month ("what drove it?") and the per-line series
   that would make the archive analytical were never published — even though
   the data (per-month stresses) was already in
   `research/backtest-results.json`, committed every day.
3. **The single biggest gap:** the site sold "23 years of data" and delivered
   one curve. This review's implementation closes that gap.

## What was shipped (commit `95cf15d`)

- **Per-line stress histories** — `scripts/build-line-history.js` publishes
  `data/line-history.json/.js` (282 months × 7 weighted lines) from the
  canonical backtest: no recomputation, no typed values; unknown lines fail
  the build loudly. Run in `collect.yml` after `backtest.js`. Indicator pages
  render 2003→today stress charts with hover readouts; auxiliary lines state
  honestly why no series exists (never a sketch).
- **Archive month dossier** — the Time Machine renders score, band, delta and
  the heaviest lines, apportioned by the same largest-remainder rule as the
  collector, plus that month's reconstruction link. Clicking the curve picks
  the month.
- **Advancing archive** — `backfill-reports.js` anchors the trailing-year
  window to the backtest's latest month (2025-07 pages retired, 2026-07 added).
- **Facility search** — Tools dropdown (desktop + mobile) over intake lines,
  articles, archive reconstructions and chart events; Enter opens, Escape
  closes, corpus is whatever data files the page loaded.
- **Clean URLs** — all chrome links canonical `/slug/` pages; `?i=` templates
  remain for deep links.
- **Grime, fixed** — the live ledger crash (share-card wiring had been dead
  since the debtBurden line landed), dead anchor, boot a11y, hero one-liner,
  market cadence note.
