# The trailing-year report corpus

Twelve months of OOZEMeter reports, read in sequence — 23 archive
reconstructions (household + Ward M per month) followed by the one report the
facility actually sealed live. Generated by scripts/compile-reports.js from
data/reconstruction-reports.js and data/editorial.json; every figure traces to
the public backtests. **Read it the way a subscriber would — in order, without
skipping** — and the identity question answers itself: what stays constant
across a year, what varies, where the formula shows, and where it earns its keep.

> **What these are.** The facility opened in July 2026. Every report below dated
> before that is a reconstruction: today's methodology run over the latest revised
> public data, labeled as such in its own first paragraph. Only the final entry —
> June 2026 — was sealed live.
