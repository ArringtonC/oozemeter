🚗 Auto Loans

Auto stress can be measured through balances, origination rates, payment amounts, delinquency transitions, serious delinquency, or repossessions. These are related but not interchangeable.

Tangible win: Choose an auto-specific stress measure and recognize when a broader consumer-loan series is only a proxy.

3 · Know OOZEMeter's transformation

The current collector uses DRCLACBS, a broad consumer-loan delinquency series, as an interim proxy. The lesson track treats it as provisional until an auto-specific NY Fed extraction is reproducible.

Interpretation trap

DRCLACBS covers consumer loans beyond autos. Labeling it “auto delinquency” without qualification overstates its specificity.
