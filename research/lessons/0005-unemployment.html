Lesson 05 · Level plus shock detector

👷 Unemployment

UNRATE estimates the share of the labor force unemployed. ICSA counts new unemployment-insurance claims. One describes labor-market level monthly; the other detects abrupt layoffs weekly.

Tangible win: Combine a slow labor-market level with a fast claims signal without treating them as the same statistic.

1 · Know the measurement

Primary measurement
UNRATE + ICSA 4W

Civilian unemployment level plus the four-week mean of initial claims

Release clock

3 · Know OOZEMeter's transformation

The recommended line calculates UNRATE stress and stress from the trailing four-week mean of ICSA separately, then uses the higher value. The current collector averages weekly claims within each score month; that implementation difference must be resolved before the production methodology is frozen.

Interpretation trap

Initial claims are not the number of unemployed people. UNRATE can also stay stable when labor-force participation changes, so definitions matter.

4 · Retrieval check
