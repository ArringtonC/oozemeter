Lesson 01 · Fast pressure signal

⛽ Gas Prices

Gas is a high-frequency, visible household expense. The number changes quickly, but OOZEMeter must separate the current pump price from the stress transformation applied to it.

Tangible win: Read a national gasoline-price observation and explain whether it increases near-term household pressure.

3 · Know OOZEMeter's transformation

The collector reads the latest weekly nominal dollars-per-gallon value, converts historical observations into current-dollar terms with CPI for comparison, and maps the result onto the published gas anchor curve.

Interpretation trap

Do not scrape AAA or imply AAA is the collector source. FRED’s GASREGW observations originate with EIA; source ownership and series notes should be displayed accurately.

4 · Retrieval check

The weekly pump price rises, but CPI rises by the same proportion over the comparison period. What changed?
