Lesson 03 · Household cushion failure

💳 Credit Cards

Balances show how much is owed. APR shows the carrying cost. Delinquency shows missed payments. Charge-offs occur after lenders conclude some debts are unlikely to be collected.

Tangible win: Interpret card delinquency as household-payment stress without confusing it with balances, APR, or charge-offs.

3 · Know OOZEMeter's transformation

The launch score maps the quarterly delinquency rate to the credit-card stress curve. Companion values such as balances and APR explain why pressure may be building but should not be silently substituted for delinquency.

Interpretation trap

Nominal card balances can set a record while remaining stable relative to income or population. A delinquency rate also depends on its exact loan and borrower denominator.

4 · Retrieval check

Card balances rise, but the delinquency rate and transition into delinquency both fall. What can OOZEMeter safely say?
