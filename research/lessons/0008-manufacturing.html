Lesson 08 · Leading/coincident auxiliary sensor

🏭 Manufacturing

Industrial production measures realized physical output. New orders measure incoming demand. PMI is a rapid survey diffusion index, but redistribution rights are not equivalent to public visibility.

Tangible win: Use free output and orders data as manufacturing sensors without republishing a licensed PMI figure.

1 · Know the measurement

Primary measurement
INDPRO + AMTMNO

Industrial Production + Manufacturers New Orders

Release clock
Monthly

Leading/coincident auxiliary sensor

3 · Know OOZEMeter's transformation

At launch, OOZEMeter can use INDPRO and AMTMNO as free, first-party proxies for manufacturing conditions while keeping the line unweighted until its transformation is validated.

Interpretation trap

A PMI value visible in news coverage is not automatically free to republish. INDPRO and orders also have different units and should not be combined without an explicit transformation.

4 · Retrieval check

New orders fall while industrial production is still rising. What is the cautious interpretation?
