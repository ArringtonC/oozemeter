Lesson 07 · Lagging distress confirmation

🏦 Foreclosures

Delinquency begins after missed payments; foreclosure is a later legal process. A delinquency series can warn of terminal housing stress but cannot count foreclosure filings.

Tangible win: Explain why mortgage delinquency is a launch proxy for foreclosure stress and where the proxy stops being valid.

3 · Know OOZEMeter's transformation

OOZEMeter can surface DRSFRMACBS as a free auxiliary sensor or housing component, explicitly labeled “mortgage delinquency proxy.” True national foreclosure filings require a different dataset.

Interpretation trap

Do not display a delinquency percentage as a foreclosure rate. Legal timelines, forbearance, equity, and state rules break any one-to-one conversion.

4 · Retrieval check

Mortgage delinquency rises this quarter. Can the site state that foreclosure filings rose by the same amount?
