📈 Inflation

CPI is a price index, not itself an inflation rate. OOZEMeter computes the percentage change from the same month one year earlier. Headline includes food and energy; core excludes them.

Tangible win: Calculate and interpret year-over-year CPI inflation while distinguishing headline from core measures.

3 · Know OOZEMeter's transformation

The recommended published measure calculates (NSA headline CPI ÷ the same month one year earlier − 1) × 100. The current collector performs the same year-over-year calculation with seasonally adjusted CPIAUCSL. The choice must be frozen and disclosed before production because seasonally adjusted history can revise.

Interpretation trap

Falling inflation means prices are rising more slowly, not usually falling. A one-month percent change and a 12-month percent change answer different questions.

4 · Retrieval check

Year-over-year inflation falls from 4% to 3%, while the CPI index rises. What happened to average prices?
