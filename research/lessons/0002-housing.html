Lesson 02 · Affordability plus distress

🏠 Housing

Housing stress has at least two clocks: mortgage rates change weekly and affect affordability quickly; delinquency changes quarterly and confirms deeper payment failure later.

Tangible win: Distinguish housing affordability pressure from housing-credit failure and choose the right data for each.

3 · Know OOZEMeter's transformation

OOZEMeter currently takes the higher stress reading from the mortgage-rate curve and the mortgage-delinquency curve. That allows fast affordability pressure or slower payment failure to dominate without adding both twice.

Interpretation trap

A high mortgage rate is not the same event as a foreclosure wave. Existing owners with fixed-rate loans may be insulated while buyers face severe affordability pressure.

4 · Retrieval check

Mortgage rates jump this week while mortgage delinquency remains low. Which statement is strongest?
