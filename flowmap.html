Blueprint 01 — System Architecture

How the facility runs itself

Four lanes. The Data Core produces the number; the Content Engine turns each day's number into publishable pages; Distribution carries them out of the building; Governance keeps every claim honest. No lane publishes a figure the Data Core didn't produce.

external

Public feeds

FRED (UNRATE, CPI, MORTGAGE30US, delinquencies) · EIA gas · NY Fed CSVs. Free, licensed, linked at source.

GitHub Actions

Collector — collect.js

Runs daily 12:00 UTC. Fetches every series, records asOf per line, flags staleness.

Scoring engine

Published anchor curves × weights → stress per line → OOZE 0–100. ICSA momentum term. One calibration: GFC = 90. OOZEMAXING breadth check (all 6 ≥ 60).

the database

data/latest.json + history.json

Committed to the repo. Git history = public audit log. Pages redeploys automatically.

Blueprint 02 — Visitor Flow & Look

How a visit works, and how each moment looks

Six stages from arrival to habit. Left: what happens. Middle: what it must accomplish. Right: how it looks and what proves it worked.

01

Arrive — the clearance

First visit of the session: the boot check types out, the room fades in, the jar fills to today's real score while the number counts up. Repeat sessions skip straight to the jar.

Entry: search · share link · newsletter · direct habit
Look

Dark lab, phosphor green on near-black. Unbounded numerals, Plex Mono labels. Motion happens once, then the liquid comes to rest. Stillness is the resting state.

Proof it worked

Score + status + delta + top driver understood in < 5 seconds, phone-first, no scroll.

02

Understand — the cascade

File 00 tells the definition as a story: gas → credit → car → job → house. Six steps, each one line of copy, each linking to its indicator file. Then the top pressure cards and today's fresh leaks.

Home → cascade → top 3 cards → movers
Look

Numbered hairline rows, no boxes-in-boxes. The OOZEMAXING callout is the only red object on the page — red is spent nowhere else, so here it lands.

Proof

"Ohhh — housing is why the jar is filling." No reading required beyond one screen.

03

Go deeper — an indicator file

One intake line, one page: current reading, 20-year chart with recession bands, why it matters, the 2008 incident comparison, FAQ, and "personnel also observe" links to the next file.

Card / node / chip / footer → /gas → related → /credit …
Look

Narrow prose column (~70ch), chart in a quiet panel, red reserved for the 2008 incident stamp. Native details/summary FAQ — no widgetry.

Proof

2+ pages per session. The related-links loop is the whole SEO/session model.

04

Explore — the restricted wings

Incident Archive (scrub to 2008, alarms fire), Personal Ooze (your own jar fills), State Rankings, Lab Notes (the full recipe), and the daily Ooze Report articles with headlines that survived the filter.

Teasers → archive / personal / states / notes / reports
Look

Each wing keeps the instrument language but earns one signature moment: the alarm wash, the second jar, the ranked pressure bars. Easter eggs stay event-driven.

Proof

Archive scrub-to-2008 is the screenshot moment; session time > 2 min.

05

Carry it out — share & subscribe

The field report copies in one tap; the OG image makes the link unfurl as the jar itself. The Morning Specimen asks for exactly one thing: an email address.

Share card → X/Reddit/Threads · Newsletter → inbox
Look

The share card is designed like the product's business card — jar, number, band color, nothing else. The signup form is two fields pretending to be a clearance request.

Proof

Share rate per session; subscriber growth; embeds in the wild. Gate 3: form must reach a real ESP first — today it captures nothing.

06

Return — the habit

New reading every morning at 8 ET. The email says what changed; the site says why. Streak counter acknowledges the ritual. Over time: alerts ("notify me above 70") and the widget on other people's sites doing the reminding.

Email / muscle memory / alert → home → repeat
Look

The delta is the hero on return visits — yesterday's mark ghosted on the jar ticks. The site should feel like checking a gauge, not reading a website.

Proof

Daily returning visitors — the only metric that means the product works.

Blueprint 05 — High-Level Requirements

What "working correctly" means, per section

Short and testable. If a change breaks one of these, it doesn't ship. Detailed specs live in REQUIREMENTS.md; these are the laws.

THE JAR

Homepage

  • HOME-1 · A first-time visitor understands the score, direction, and top driver in under 5 seconds, on a phone
  • HOME-2 · The jar's fill level always equals the displayed number
  • HOME-3 · Motion settles when nothing is changing; alarms only at real thresholds
DATA

Feeds & cadence

  • DATA-1 · Every displayed number traces to a linked public source
  • DATA-2 · The monthly headline seals once per month, when jobs + CPI land
  • DATA-3 · Each line refreshes on its own release schedule and shows its own as-of date
  • DATA-4 · A quiet feed gets flagged STALE — never silently reused
ALGO

Scoring

  • ALGO-1 · No black boxes: anchors, weights, and calibration are published
  • ALGO-2 · The scale is absolute — 90+ stays depression-class forever
  • ALGO-3 · Anyone can rerun the script and get the same score
  • ALGO-4 · Recipe changes are announced, never silent
TRUTH

Honesty

  • TRUTH-1 · No simulated or estimated number ever renders without a label
  • TRUTH-2 · If data is missing, the site says OFFLINE — it never invents
  • TRUTH-3 · Every historical claim survives a check against the raw series
ARCHIVE

History

  • ARCH-1 · The replay curve is computed from data, never hand-drawn
  • ARCH-2 · Incident files carry real dates (NBER) and backtested peaks
TOOLS

Calculator & states

  • TOOL-1 · Personal numbers never leave the visitor's browser
  • TOOL-2 · Simulated placeholders (states) say so on the page
CONTENT

Articles (Gate 4)

  • CONT-1 · Every article's numbers come from data/ — grounded, never a news rewrite
  • CONT-2 · Generated pages are stamped from the golden-master template
  • CONT-3 · The refutation check runs before anything publishes
GROWTH

Distribution

  • GROW-1 · Every page links deeper into the facility (no dead ends)
  • GROW-2 · Sharing anything takes one tap
  • GROW-3 · No feature ships without knowing how it will be measured
MEASURE

Measurement law

  • MEAS-1 · Every new section ships with its metric, its target, and its removal condition — or it doesn't ship
  • MEAS-2 · A feature that can't be measured yet states what will measure it and when
FUN

Delight

  • FUN-1 · The protected inventory (bloops, glass tap, alarms, boot) survives every cleanup
  • FUN-2 · Every release answers "is it still fun?" before shipping
  • FUN-3 · Playful never trivializes real hardship
