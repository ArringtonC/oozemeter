What is ooze?

Ooze is economic stress moving through a household's defenses — in a fixed order. Every budget fails the same way, one line at a time. The meter reads how far down the cascade the average American household is.

01

⛽ Gas gets expensive

The first pressure every budget feels — posted in foot-tall numbers, paid twice a week, impossible to skip.

☢ OOZEMAXING

When the whole chain fails at once. Not one line under pressure — every line, everywhere, feeding each other: gas, credit, cars, jobs, and housing failing together across the economy, the way it happened in the Great Depression and in the spring of 2020.

It's the ceiling of the meter. Formally: OOZEMAXING is declared when every weighted intake line reads elevated at the same time — a breadth condition, not just a high score. In the comparable record since 2003 it has never been met. If this facility ever declares it, it will not be hype.
