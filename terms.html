Terms of Use

Effective 2026-08-02. Short version: this is economics education wearing a lab coat. Learn from it, share it, verify it — just don't trade on it or republish it as your own.

What OOZEMeter is

OOZEMeter is an educational visualization of U.S. economic conditions computed from public data. It is not financial, investment, legal, or tax advice; not a forecast; and not a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold anything. The facility's own rule is printed on its instruments: it measures, does not prophesy. Decisions you make from what you read here are yours.

Accuracy, honestly stated

Every number traces to a cited public source, the methodology is published, and an integrity gate checks readings before publication — and still: sources revise their history, collection can fail, and the methodology itself is versioned and changes on the public record (policies). The site is provided "as is," without warranty of any kind. We fix errors under the published correction policy rather than pretending they can't happen.

What you may do

  • Cite the score — quote the Ooze Level or Ward M reading with attribution ("OOZEMeter") and, where practical, a link.
  • Share — the share cards, links, and report text exist to be posted anywhere.
  • Verify and rebuild — the code and pipeline are public; recomputing our numbers from the cited sources is encouraged, not tolerated.

What you may not do

  • Republish substantial portions of the site's content or data files as your own work, or strip attribution from the score when quoting it.
  • Present OOZEMeter's numbers as endorsements of any product, investment, or political position.
  • Misrepresent modified numbers as OOZEMeter readings — the score's integrity is the entire product.
  • Abuse the site or its hosting (scraping at hostile rates, disrupting availability).
