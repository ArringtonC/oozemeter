The Ooze Formula

No black boxes in this facility. Every point of ooze is explainable and reproducible — this page is the complete recipe.

How the specimen is measured

OOZE = Σ ( weightᵢ × stressᵢ )

Each indicator is scored 0–100 against fixed, published anchor points (unemployment at 25% = 100; the calmest modern month = 10; the 2008 peak = 90), then multiplied by its weight. The month's contributions sum to the headline — currently {TOTAL} ({MONTH}).

Cadence: the headline seals once a month, when that month's jobs report and CPI land (~mid-month — the earliest moment all six lines exist). Individual intake lines refresh whenever their source releases: gas and mortgage rates weekly, jobs and inflation monthly, delinquencies quarterly. Each line carries its own as-of date, and a line gone quiet past its normal cadence is flagged STALE rather than silently reused.

Weights are fixed and published. Every chart links to its original government or industry source. If we change the recipe, we tell you.

☢ OOZEMAXING — a separate breadth condition, not a score band: declared only when every weighted intake line reads 60+ simultaneously — gas, credit, autos, jobs, housing, and inflation all failing at once, feeding each other. Depression-class. Never met in the comparable record since 2003: at the GFC's worst (June 2009), five of six lines crossed 60 — credit 90, housing 87, autos 85, employment 78, gas 62 — and only deflation, holding the inflation line at 55, kept the declaration off the board.
