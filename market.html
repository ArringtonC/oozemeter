What this ward is — and is not

Ward M is an experimental market-stress instrument, opened after the facility's market-signal review. Some financial conditions can move before household outcomes, but this same-month comparison does not establish a lead, forecast, or causal relationship. Markets also cry wolf: they have panicked plenty of times while households stayed fine. So the ward is kept in a separate wing, on its own scale, with its own seal. Nothing here changes the Ooze Score.

Sector Watch sits under the composite because its eleven ticker proxies are transparent, but they mix broad indexes, sectors, and industries with material overlap. The live panel uses Yahoo's undocumented quote.close transport; it is a price-return reconstruction, not total return, and derived-only publication does not resolve quote rights. Scheduled collection remains disabled pending a licensed or explicitly permitted source. The other five gauges come from government, Federal Reserve, or Cboe series with their original publishers identified.

The composite is the average of six gauges — rates, volatility, credit & funding, energy, the dollar, and market breadth from Sector Watch (so a weakening ticker proxy moves this score) — calibrated to the ward's own 2007–present history on the same doctrine as the jar: the ward's calmest month reads 10, its November 2008 crisis peak reads 90. The two instruments share no data and genuinely disagree when history disagrees — the ward screamed through 2020 and late 2022 while the household jar stayed comparatively calm, which is the divergence this wing exists to show. Gauge anchors remain provisional — the full anchor tables live in the collector source and are percentile-checked against each gauge's full history in the anchor-validation report — and will be revisited after the ward's first live tightening episode. Four earlier sensors — builders, industry, freight, and the bitcoin canary — are parked in the improvements ledger to keep the ward readable; their anchors are preserved for when more data earns its place.

The full decision record — why the market is displayed beside the jar rather than blended into it, four backtested studies included — is public in the market-signal review. The Credit & Funding sensor (Chicago Fed NFCI) is the designated bridge for methodology v3, which will add it to the flagship at 3% under the disclosures recorded there.
